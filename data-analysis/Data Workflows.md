# Data Workflows

The **data analysis process** describes the work of the analyst. The **data life cycle** describes how data is handled from planning through disposal.

## Data Analysis Process

Revisit earlier phases as new questions, findings, or data-quality issues arise.

1. **Ask:** Define the problem and understand stakeholders’ expectations.
2. **Prepare:** Select, collect, and store suitable data for the analysis, checking its sources, relevance, and access requirements.
3. **Process:** Clean the data by identifying and correcting errors and inaccuracies.
   - Fix typos and inconsistencies.
   - Transform data and combine datasets as needed.
   - Investigate outliers to determine whether they reflect errors or valid observations, then document how they are handled.
4. **Analyze:** Organize, transform, and examine the data to draw conclusions, make predictions where appropriate, and support informed decision-making.
5. **Share:** Interpret and communicate the results, using visualizations where helpful.
6. **Act:** Use the findings to make decisions and take action to address the problem.

## Data Life Cycle

1. **Plan:** Decide what data is needed and how it will be managed.
2. **Capture:** Collect the data from its sources.
3. **Manage:** Store, secure, and maintain the data.
4. **Analyze:** Use the data to draw conclusions, solve problems, and support decisions.
5. **Archive:** Store data that is no longer actively used for future reference.
6. **Destroy:** Securely delete or dispose of data that is no longer needed.
