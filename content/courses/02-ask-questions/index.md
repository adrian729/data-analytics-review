This course is the Ask phase in depth: how to frame a problem, recognize which of six problem types you're solving, and turn a vague ask into SMART questions — then how to tell data-driven decisions from data-inspired ones and share findings through dashboards.

The second half is practical — spreadsheet formulas, functions, and the errors that trip people up — and closes with the structured-thinking habits and stakeholder-management skills that keep a project on track once real people and real deadlines are involved.
