| | [Small data](/glossary#small-data) | [Big data](/glossary#big-data) |
|---|---|---|
| Time period | Short, well-defined | Long |
| Specificity | Specific metrics | Large, less-specific datasets |
| Where it lives | Usually a spreadsheet | Usually a database, queried |
| Who uses it | Small and midsize businesses | Large organizations |
| Effort to collect/store/manage | Simple | Takes a lot of effort |
| Size for analysis | Usually already manageable | Usually needs breaking into smaller pieces |

## Big data's challenges and benefits

Challenges: data overload and irrelevant information; important data buried and harder to find, slowing decisions; needed data not always accessible; tooling that still struggles to give measurable, reportable data (a source of unfair algorithmic bias); gaps in big-data business solutions.

Benefits: spotting more efficient ways to do business; surfacing customer buying-pattern and satisfaction trends that inform new products; a better read on current market conditions; tracking online presence and feedback to protect the brand.

## The (three or four) Vs of big data

| V | What it describes |
|---|---|
| **Volume** | The amount of data |
| **Variety** | The different kinds of data |
| **Velocity** | How fast the data can be processed |
| **Veracity** *(sometimes added as a 4th V)* | The quality and reliability of the data |
