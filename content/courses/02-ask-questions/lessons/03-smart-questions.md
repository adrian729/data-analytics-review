Your data won't tell you much if you don't start with the right questions. Highly effective questions follow the [SMART methodology](/glossary#smart-methodology):

| Letter | Question to ask yourself |
|---|---|
| **[Specific](/glossary#specific-question)** | Does it address the problem? Does it have context? Will it uncover the information you need? |
| **[Measurable](/glossary#measurable-question)** | Will it give you answers you can measure? |
| **[Action-oriented](/glossary#action-oriented-question)** | Will the answers help you devise a plan? |
| **[Relevant](/glossary#relevant-question)** | Is it about the particular problem you're trying to solve? |
| **[Time-bound](/glossary#time-bound-question)** | Are the answers relevant to the specific time being studied? |

## Worked example

Problem: *"What features do people look for when buying a new car?"* → possible SMART questions:

- On a scale of 1–10, how important is four-wheel drive? Explain.
- What are the top five features you'd like to see in a car package?
- What features, if included with four-wheel drive, would make you more inclined to buy?
- How does four-wheel drive contribute to a car's value, in your opinion?

Questions should be open-ended — that's what gets responses detailed enough to qualify or disqualify a potential solution.

## Question types to avoid

| Type | Example | Better version |
|---|---|---|
| **[Leading question](/glossary#leading-question)** — suggests an answer as part of the question | "This product is too expensive, isn't it?" | "What price would make you consider purchasing this product?" |
| **Closed-ended question** — invites a one-word response | "Were you satisfied with the customer trial?" | "What did you learn about customer experience from the trial?" |
| **Vague question** — lacks specificity or context | "Does the tool work for you?" | "Is the new tool faster, slower, or the same as the old one for data entry? By how much?" |
| **[Unfair question](/glossary#unfair-question)** — makes an assumption, or is otherwise hard to answer honestly | "What do you love most about our exhibits?" (assumes the visitor loves them) | "What's your honest reaction to the exhibits?" |
