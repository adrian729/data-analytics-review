| When | Checklist |
|---|---|
| **Before** | Identify the objective — purpose, goals, and questions to address. Acknowledge participants and involve their different points of view and experience. Organize the data to present (visualize raw data if needed). Prepare and distribute an agenda ahead of time so attendees can prepare questions or feedback. |
| **During** | Make introductions if needed and review key messages. Present the data, then discuss observations, interpretations, and implications. Take notes. Summarize next steps before the meeting ends. |
| **After** | Distribute notes and data. Confirm next steps and a timeline. Ask for feedback — it's the fastest way to catch anything your recap missed. |

## What a good agenda includes

- Start and end time
- Location (and how to join remotely, if applicable)
- Objectives
- Background material or data participants should review beforehand

If a meeting goes off track — an emergency comes up, a decision changes requirements mid-stream, an action item stops applying — it's fine to shorten or cancel it. Just loop in your manager or stakeholders on what changed, and reschedule once you have more information.
