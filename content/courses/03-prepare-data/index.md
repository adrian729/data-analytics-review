This course is where data stops being an abstract concept and becomes something you choose, judge, and structure: what data types and formats exist, how to tell a biased dataset from a credible one, and what data ethics and privacy demand from the way you collect it.

The second half moves into databases — relational structure, metadata, keys, SQL syntax for querying large datasets — and closes with organizing and securing your own data, plus building the professional network you'll rely on once you're doing this for real.
