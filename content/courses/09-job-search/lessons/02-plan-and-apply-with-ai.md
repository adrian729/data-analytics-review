An ad hoc search burns time and confidence; this module's throughline, as the notes describe it, is to make the search explicit — a written **[job search plan](/glossary#job-search-plan)**, a **[resume](/glossary#resume)** tailored per role with AI support, and a personalized **[cover letter](/glossary#cover-letter)** checked against a recruiter's scan — using Gemini and Career Dreamer as drafting help, not as autopilot.

## Build a job search plan with Gemini — cheatsheet

| Step | Do this | Gemini prompt shape |
|---|---|---|
| 1 — Targets | List 3–5 role titles, industries, and locations you will actually apply to | "Given these transferable skills [paste], suggest 5 entry-level analytics titles and why each fits" |
| 2 — Channels | Name where you will find roles: job boards, company sites, referrals, community boards | "What are the trade-offs of [platform list] for entry-level analytics in [city]?" |
| 3 — Weekly rhythm | Block time for search, tailoring, outreach, and skill practice | "Draft a 4-week job search plan with 10 hrs/week, 60% applications / 25% networking / 15% upskilling" |
| 4 — Tracking | Create a sheet to log every application (see lesson 04) | "Generate a Sheets column list for a job search tracker" |
| 5 — Review | Revisit weekly: what moved, what stalled, what to adjust | "Turn these tracker notes into 3 adjustments for next week" |

A **[job search plan](/glossary#job-search-plan)** is valuable because it is explicit and revisable; without it, effort scatters across unrelated postings and follow-ups slip.

## Resume best practices and tailoring with AI — cheatsheet

A **[resume](/glossary#resume)** in these notes is a one-page, role-specific summary of relevant accomplishments, not a full history; structure is header, tailored summary, skills, experience with outcome bullets (I accomplished X as measured by Y doing Z), and education/certificate, kept scannable for recruiters and applicant tracking systems. The AI workflow is: paste the posting and your bullet into Gemini, ask for missing keywords, rewrite one bullet at a time to mirror the posting while staying truthful, then verify every claim; your [portfolio](/glossary#portfolio) link belongs in the header.

## The personalized cover letter — cheatsheet

A **[cover letter](/glossary#cover-letter)** is not a resume repeat; it is a short letter that names the role, connects one or two accomplishments to the team's need, and shows why this organization. Prompt Gemini with the posting, your example, and a tone request ("concise, professional, specific"), then edit so each paragraph earns its place — the [elevator pitch](/glossary#elevator-pitch) often supplies the opening line.
