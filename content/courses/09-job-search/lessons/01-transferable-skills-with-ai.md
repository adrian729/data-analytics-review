The notes describe this as the course's shortest module (~1 hour); it uses AI not to invent experience but to name what you already have: **[transferable skills](/glossary#transferable-skills)** are the problem-solving habits you carry between roles, and Gemini paired with **[Career Dreamer](/glossary#career-dreamer)** helps surface them from plain-language descriptions of what you have actually done so far, which you then compress into a **[career identity statement](/glossary#career-identity-statement)** that anchors the rest of the search.

## What counts as a transferable skill

A **[transferable skills](/glossary#transferable-skills)** set is not a credential list but a behavior list — explaining a complex process to a newcomer, spotting why numbers do not add up, keeping a team aligned under a deadline, or documenting a decision so others can follow it — and the notes frame the first exercise as writing out past jobs, volunteer work, and school projects in everyday language so the underlying habits become visible apart from job titles.

## Mapping experience with Gemini and Career Dreamer

The workflow the notes describe is deliberately low-friction: paste a few sentences about a previous role into Gemini, ask it to extract skills and to suggest how those skills map to entry-level analytics tasks, then open **[Career Dreamer](/glossary#career-dreamer)** to explore adjacent roles and see which skills recur across them; the goal is not to accept an AI label uncritically but to get a draft vocabulary — [analytical skills](/glossary#analytical-skills), collaboration, attention to detail — that you then edit for accuracy before it appears anywhere public.

## The career identity statement

A **[career identity statement](/glossary#career-identity-statement)** is a two-to-three-sentence answer to who you are professionally, what value you bring, and what direction you are headed — for example, that you are a detail-oriented problem solver who translates messy operations into clear processes and now applies that habit to data cleaning and reporting — and the notes present Career Dreamer as the launch point for drafting it, with Gemini as the editor that tightens phrasing and checks that each claim ties to a concrete example you could discuss with [stakeholders](/glossary#stakeholders).
