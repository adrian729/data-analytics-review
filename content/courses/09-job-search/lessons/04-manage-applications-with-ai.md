## Job platforms — quick overview

Job platforms differ in who posts and how you are surfaced: large aggregators (Indeed, LinkedIn Jobs) maximize volume and filters; company career pages are authoritative for a specific employer; community boards surface local roles with less competition. The generally-true move is to pick one primary platform to check daily and one secondary to check weekly, and to record where each lead came from so you can see which channel actually converts.

## Build a job search tracker with Gemini + Sheets — cheatsheet

| Column | What to log | Validation tip |
|---|---|---|
| Company / Role | Exact posting title and employer | Copy link to posting before it expires |
| Source | Platform or referral name | Lets you compare hit rates by channel |
| Date applied | When you submitted | Use ISO dates so sorting works |
| Status | Applied → Screening → Interview → Offer / Closed | Single-select, not free text |
| Contacts | Names, emails, next-step date | Follow up within a week if silent |
| Materials | Resume version + cover letter + portfolio link sent | Note tailoring tweaks per role |
| Notes | What you learned, what you would change | Powers weekly plan review |

Prompt shape: "Generate a Google Sheets header row and data validation rules for a job search tracker with these columns [paste], plus conditional formatting that highlights rows past 7 days without follow-up." Paste your real rows afterward; ask Gemini to flag missing next steps or duplicate companies before you share the sheet. A **[job search tracker](/glossary#job-search-tracker)** earns its keep only when you review it weekly and reconcile it against [data validation](/glossary#data-validation) habits — stale statuses are the tracker equivalent of dirty data.

## Drafting networking outreach with Gemini — cheatsheet

**[Professional networking](/glossary#professional-networking)** in the notes is not mass-messaging but brief, specific outreach to people whose work you genuinely follow. Drafting pattern: "Draft a 4-sentence LinkedIn message to [name] about [specific post/project], mention [shared interest], ask one small, answerable question, no ask for a job." Include the thread link, keep it under 100 words, and personalize one detail only you would know; Gemini supplies phrasing, you supply relevance, and the contact is then logged in the tracker with a follow-up date so [stakeholders](/glossary#stakeholders) hear from you once, clearly, rather than repeatedly and vaguely.
