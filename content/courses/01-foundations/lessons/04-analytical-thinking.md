Two lists that sound alike and are not. [Analytical skills](/glossary#analytical-skills) are the qualities you bring; [analytical thinking](/glossary#analytical-thinking) is the process you apply.

## The five core analytical skills

| Skill | What it means |
|---|---|
| **Curiosity** | Wanting to learn and seek out new information. |
| **Understanding [context](/glossary#context)** | Recognizing the condition in which something exists — what belongs and what doesn't in a given situation. |
| **[Technical mindset](/glossary#technical-mindset)** | Breaking a task into smaller, ordered, logical steps. |
| **[Data design](/glossary#data-design)** | How information is organized for clarity and usability. |
| **[Data strategy](/glossary#data-strategy)** | Managing the people, processes, and tools used in the analysis. |

Data design and data strategy are the pair most easily confused: **design is about the information, strategy is about the operation around it.**

## The five aspects of analytical thinking

1. **Visualization** — the graphical representation of data. Visuals communicate patterns faster than raw numbers or words.
2. **Strategy** — staying focused on what the data actually needs to accomplish.
3. **Problem-orientation** — keeping the real problem, not its symptoms, at the center of the work for the whole project.
4. **[Correlation](/glossary#correlation)** — identifying a relationship between two or more variables. The caveat matters more than the definition: **correlation does not equal causation.** Two trends moving together does not prove that one causes the other.
5. **Big-picture and detail-oriented thinking** — seeing the overall pattern *and* the individual components. Most people are naturally stronger at one; the job needs both.

## Getting to the cause

The [root cause](/glossary#root-cause) is the actual reason a problem occurs — not the symptom that made it visible. The course names two techniques for finding it.

**The [Five Whys](/glossary#five-whys)** — ask "why" repeatedly, conventionally five times, each answer digging one layer deeper into the causal chain, until the root cause is exposed rather than the surface complaint.

**[Gap analysis](/glossary#gap-analysis)** — examine the current state of a process against a desired future state, and identify what has to change to close the distance between them. Where the Five Whys works backward from a problem, gap analysis works forward from a goal.
