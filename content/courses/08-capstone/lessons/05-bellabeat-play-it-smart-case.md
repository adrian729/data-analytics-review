Bellabeat is the second supplied Track A scenario, and its pairing with the open-ended Track B template is the course's way of showing one outline can produce two different portfolios — an employer-prompted use of given data versus a self-prompted hunt for data that fits a personal question.

## Bellabeat: wellness-company business task and dataset

Bellabeat, a high-tech manufacturer of health-focused products for women, asks for analysis of publicly available smart-device data to understand how consumers actually use wearables and to guide future marketing strategy. The packet, like Cyclistic, expects a full run of the [data analysis process](/glossary#data-analysis-process) — frame the [business task](/glossary#business-task), prepare and vet the device data, process and document cleaning, analyze for usage trends, share with clear visuals, and act with marketing recommendations — ending in a portfolio-ready [report](/glossary#report) or presentation. The scenario is intentionally narrower on domain but identical on structure, so a recruiter can compare two candidates' process discipline even when the datasets differ.

## The reusable outline and its self-directed variant

The same Track B outline underlies case study 3: follow your own case study path, choose a business question that interests you, find a dataset, create the task and audience definition, and repeat the six phases. The repository's own Track B illustration chooses COVID-19 as the illustration: define a multi-metric business task spanning infection, mortality, vaccination, and economic indicators; prepare data from Johns Hopkins University and Our World in Data with daily counts, demographics, and economic series; and explicitly document preparation decisions. That walk-through does not yet contain a finished cleaning script or final visualizations — its value as an example is earlier, showing how to phrase the question broadly enough to support recommendations for public-health policy and economic recovery while staying answerable with the sourced tables, and where to leave room for documented integration and credibility checks.

## What both paths model for a finished case study

Viewed together, Bellabeat and the COVID-19 sketch teach portfolio pragmatics: a strong case study states purpose, assumptions, and needed deliverables up front, organizes analysis artifacts so each phase's key tasks are visible, and closes by choosing a recommended solution with who should do what and when. Whether the data is the supplied fitness-tracker extract or a self-sourced public series, the narrative obligation is unchanged — explain why the question matters to a stakeholder, show what you did to the data and why, and make the business implication impossible to miss.
