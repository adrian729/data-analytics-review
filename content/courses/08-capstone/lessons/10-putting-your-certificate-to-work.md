Completion is framed as a showcase moment: after the capstone, translate verified skills into visible assets recruiters can find and trust.

## Showcase your work on resume and LinkedIn

Add the Certificate to resume and LinkedIn immediately using templates from Enhancv, Big Interview, Google Docs, and Microsoft Word plus Applied Digital Skills' Start a Resume guidance.

Update Education or Licenses and Certifications on the resume; on LinkedIn add it to Licenses and Certifications. Refresh Skills with program capabilities: data analysis, data cleaning, data visualization, R programming, data-driven decision making, calculation and analysis in SQL and spreadsheets, and effective presentations. Update Summary or About — example: "Versatile individual with experience collecting, transforming, and organizing data for analysis. Proficient with SQL, spreadsheets, Tableau, and R. Strong communication, organizational, and analytical skills. Recently completed the Google Data Analytics Certificate — a rigorous, hands-on program covering the data analysis process."

Sharing the case study or portfolio alongside these updates signals breadth across the six phases.

## Claim your badge

Complete all eight courses to earn a digital badge from Credly via Acclaim, a credential provider employers can verify instantly and you can share to LinkedIn.

Claim it via the Credly email within about a week of completion (check spam), opt in to share information, create an Acclaim account if needed, and add it to LinkedIn via the Credly article or Vimeo tutorial — contact Coursera Learner Services if the invite does not arrive after a week.

## Explore professional opportunities and expand expertise

The video directs graduates to the end-of-certificate checklist and linked job platform to create a profile highlighting the credential — exclusive, time-limited offers.

Two advanced paths are contrasted: the Google Business Intelligence Certificate teaches processes and channels that turn raw data into actionable decisions via dashboards and pipelines with BigQuery, Tableau, and SQL in one to two months, fitting roles that measure performance and track revenue; the Google Advanced Data Analytics Certificate teaches machine learning, predictive modeling, and experimental design with Jupyter Notebook, Python, and Tableau in three to six months, fitting analyst and scientist roles that model likelihoods and causes.

Business intelligence builds tangible solutions stakeholders use daily; advanced analytics experiments with models to generate new insights — choose based on whether you preferred SQL and visualizations or programming and math.

## Resources available for graduates

The checklist reiterates: share the badge and post with #GrowwithGoogle; follow Grow with Google on LinkedIn; for U.S. graduates access CareerCircle for resume, interview prep, coaching, and job postings; practice with Interview Warmup; use Big Interview free for one year for mock interviews and templates; and share your story via the survey — all accessed with your Coursera registration email.
