This is the Share phase: turning finished analysis into something an audience can actually act on. It starts with visualization itself — why visual forms communicate where raw numbers don't, the anatomy of an effective chart from headline to annotation, the standard chart types and when each fits, and the design principles (balance, emphasis, movement, rhythm) that separate a clear visual from a cluttered one, including accessibility for audiences who need screen readers and color-safe palettes.

The course then moves from principles to practice: Tableau, from building a first visualization and choosing among its many chart types to linking datasets that live in separate places. Finally it covers the human layer — crafting a data story around the analysis, building a live dashboard, and presenting the result with a framework, handling caveats and objections, and running the Q&A that follows. It is the capstone skill of the certificate: findings mean nothing until someone else understands them.
